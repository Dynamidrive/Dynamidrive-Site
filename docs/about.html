Made For Drivers

Almost everyone drives while listening to their favorite music. From road trips to casual city driving, we are all connected by the music that we listen to while traveling.

But we wanted to take this idea further. What if your music could dynamically adapt to how you drive? What if the music can be adaptive, like it was made for the car? So we created Dynamidrive, the music player that changes your music based on your driving.

Private By Design

We don't want to see where you are or what your driving habits are. Your location data is not stored anywhere. Plus, we know you've seen enough ads elsewhere, and that's why Dynamidrive has no ads, not even without a subscription. All of the soundtracks you make are stored on your iPhone, so you can still play and create soundtracks while offline, and do so privately.
