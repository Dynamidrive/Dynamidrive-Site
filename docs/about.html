About Dynamidrive

Made For Drivers

Almost everyone drives while listening to their favorite music. From road trips to casual city driving, we are all connected by the music that we listen to while travelling.


We wanted to take this idea further. What if your music could dynamically adapt to your driving? What if music was adaptive, like it was made for a car? This is why we created Dynamidrive, the music player that changes your music, based on your driving. Inspired by video games, Dynamidrive seperates your music into dynamic tracks, and lets you choose when instruments fade in, set loops that trigger at certain speeds, and even seperate audio into stems directly on your iPhone. We've added a host of features that add to your experience, such as, Boost Mode, which gives your music a boost when you accelerate, a fully customizable app background with racing stripes to extend your car's feel onto the app, and a full screen speedometer experience that lets you choose from a set of gauge styles to let you focus on the road.


For privacy, we don't want to see where you are, or how you drive. Your location data is not stored anywhere, and we know that you have seen enough ads elsewhere. Dynamidrive has NO ads, regardless of whether you have the subscription or not.
