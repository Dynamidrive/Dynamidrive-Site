Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I make a soundtrack? There are currently 3 ways you can create a soundtrack, which are all available in the Create Soundtrack menu:
    1. Using our free audio seperator, which seperates an audio file you provide into the Drums, Bass, Voice, and Other tracks.
    2. If you have your own seperate audio files, you can upload them manually.
    3. If someone has shared a soundtrack or playlist with you, it can be imported.*
  • What is the Audio Seperator?

    The Audio Seperator is an on-device AI model that analyzes a provided MP3 file and seperates it into 4 tracks: Drums, Bass, Vocals, and Other.

  • How can I get the MP3 file of a song?

    You can find MP3 files online. Some artists provide them for free, and some require payment. Another theoretical way that we don't recommend probably is going to a video platform (like YouTube), searching for a song, copying the link, and pasting it into a YouTube to MP3 website. We don't recommend using cnvmp3.com, as it may work and go against YouTube's TOS.

  • Why aren't MP3 files provided?

    We can't provide music files for you due to copyright laws and licensing fees.

  • What is included with Dynamidrive Plus?

    Dynamidrive Plus adds: Unlimited soundtracks, unlimited tracks, soundtrack looping, soundtrack and playlist sharing, custom colors, and more to come.

  • When will Dynamidrive be on the App Store?

    Hopefully soon! We're actively working on Dynamidrive, and believe it's getting very close to a state that can be released onto the App Store.

*Requires a Dynamidrive Plus subscription.
